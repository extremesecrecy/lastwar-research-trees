Official Rules

  1. Leadership sets the current round and advancing players in the control sheet.
  2. This page automatically reads the current round and who advanced from the sheet.
  3. Enter your name and roll — you need to match the round number (Round 1 = need a 1, Round 2 = need a 2, etc.)
  4. Leadership checks results, updates the sheet with who passed, and sets the next round.
  5. Click “Refresh Status” to see the latest round and advancing players.
  6. If only ONE person passes a round — that person WINS and the game is OVER!
  7. You get ONE roll per round. No re-rolls. All results logged with UTC timestamps.
