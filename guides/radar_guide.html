Stacking Math & Golden Rule
Fill Time Formula
(Max Stored - 1) ÷ Per 6hrs × 6 = Hours
Example at Lv.9:
(35 - 1) ÷ 11 × 6 = 18.5 hours
Every level fills in 14–24 hours.
Start stacking the night before!
The Golden Rule
NEVER let storage hit max!
At max, 6-hour refresh STOPS.
Safe limit = Max - 1
Lv.8: keep at 34/35
Lv.10: keep at 39/40
Lv.17: keep at 44/45
Hit max? Collect 1 to restart timer.
When to Stack & Collect
Radar scores on 3 VS days. Stack the night before each.
STACK 1
SUN → MON
Stop Sun night
Collect Mon reset +5m
STACK 2
TUE → WED
Stop Tue night
Collect Wed reset +5m
STACK 3
THU → FRI
Stop Thu night
Collect Fri reset +5m
Non-stacking days (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat): Do radar normally. Next scoring day is too far to refill.
Step-by-Step Stacking
1
Night before scoring day — STOP collecting. Let red dots pile up. Keep completing map tasks, just don't tap to collect.
2
Watch storage counter — Stay at max-1. If at limit, stop completing until after reset.
3
Overnight — Missions generate every 6hrs while you sleep. Storage not full = timer keeps running.
4
After reset + 5 min — Collect ALL at once. Each = ~25K VS pts. Full stack = ~750K–1M+ points burst.
5
Rest of day — Do radar normally. Complete and collect for bonus points throughout the day.

Quick Reference Rules
DO
  • Keep storage at max - 1 so timer never stops
  • Start stacking night before every scoring day
  • Wait 5 min after reset before collecting
  • Complete map tasks during stacking — don't collect red dots
  • Stack 3x/week: Sun→Mon, Tue→Wed, Thu→Fri
  • Collect normally on non-scoring days
DON'T
  • NEVER let storage hit max — timer stops!
  • Don't collect red dots during stacking
  • Don't stack Mon/Wed/Fri nights
  • Don't collect within 5 min of reset
  • Don't ignore storage counter at bedtime
  • Don't leave map missions sitting — they expire ~8hrs
