1. What is the Profession Hall?

The Profession Hall is a building that unlocks in Season 1. Inside, you choose one of two professions — Engineer or War Leader — and invest skill points as you level up. Each profession has a unique skill tree with passive and active abilities that permanently boost your account.

  • Access: Season 1 event menu → Profession, or tap the Profession Hall building
  • Three options: Engineer, War Leader, or Diplomat (Diplomat not yet available)
  • Not permanent: You can switch professions using a Profession Change Certificate (resets all skills)
  • Building actions: Professions (choose/view), Profession Skills (allocate points), Building Info

2. How Leveling Works

You earn 1 skill point per profession level. New skills unlock every 5 levels. Each skill can be upgraded from Level 1 to its max (usually 5). You can also gain up to 10 bonus skill point items per season from events and rewards.

  • How to earn XP: Kill monsters on the World Map, capture cities, upgrade seasonal buildings, use Profession EXP Books
  • Excess XP: Once you hit the cap, excess converts to storage and transfers via mail at the next season’s start
  • Seasonal skills (green background in-game) reset each season. Skill points spent on them are refunded.

6. Which Profession Should You Choose?

Engineer

  • Best for F2P and development-focused players
  • Construction & research speedups save real days of building time
  • Resource cost reduction (-5% build, -5% research) compounds massively
  • Cooperative Construction/Research buffs help your entire alliance
  • Extra stamina (+50%) = more radar missions, attacks, resource gathering
  • Buddy Shield = free 4hr shield for you or an ally

War Leader

  • Best for rally callers, R4/R5 officers, and PvP fighters
  • Team Strike boosts ALL squads in your rallies
  • Hospital Prep + Intensive Training = faster war recovery
  • Charge Banner = +2% alliance-wide attack boost during wars
  • Decay Ray has a chance to permanently downgrade enemy units when attacking
  • Base Strike & Hold the Base = +5% hero stats (offense and defense)
OWOW Recommendation
Most OWOW members should go Engineer. R4/R5 rally leaders should consider War Leader.
You can switch later with a Profession Change Certificate. Your first pick is not permanent — but switching resets all skill points, so choose wisely.

8. Alliance Team Composition

A strong alliance needs both professions working together. Engineers buff the alliance’s growth, War Leaders buff the alliance’s combat power.

Role Profession Why
Most Members (70-80%) Engineer Cooperative Construction/Research sharing lifts the entire alliance. Resource savings compound. Buddy Shields protect during wars.
Rally Leaders / R4-R5 (20-30%) War Leader Team Strike buffs ALL squads in rallies. Charge Banner provides alliance-wide attack boost. Their combat skills scale with rally size.
Alliance Synergy
Engineers share Cooperative Construction/Research buffs daily — coordinate with alliance members to maximize coverage!
The Cooperative buffs do NOT stack. One Engineer buffs one ally. More Engineers = more people get buffed each day. War Leaders place Charge Banners during rallies for +2% attack.

9. How to Level Fast

  • Kill monsters on the World Map — Primary XP source. Use Combat Experience skill first (+60% XP).
  • Upgrade seasonal buildings — Big XP chunks. Use Building Inspiration (+15% bonus XP).
  • Capture cities — XP from durability damage. Siege Inspiration adds +60% XP from city fights.
  • Profession EXP Books — From events, Battle Pass, rewards. Save them for after you unlock XP-boosting skills.
  • Professional Insights (Lv 35 seasonal) — Free 10% of current level’s XP every 24 hours.
  • Excess XP carries over — Once maxed, surplus converts at a ratio and mails to you at next season start.
Pro Tip
Skill Combat Experience (3/3) and Building Inspiration I (3/3) FIRST — they’re seasonal and accelerate everything else.
Don’t use Profession EXP Books until you have these skills active. The bonus XP applies to books too.

10. Skill Reset & Profession Change

Item What It Does Source
Profession Skill Reset Book Resets all current profession skills. Refunds all skill points. Does NOT change your profession. Light/electricity rewards, events
Profession Change Certificate Switches you to the other profession. Resets ALL skills and refunds all points. Events, Battle Pass, special offers
Seasonal Skill Reset Happens automatically at end of each season. All seasonal (green) skills reset and points are refunded. Automatic

Plan your skills carefully. Reset items are limited — you may only get 1-2 per season. Don’t waste points on skills you won’t use just because they look cool.
