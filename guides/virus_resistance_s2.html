Stronghold / dig-site bosses are OWOW-confirmed (Gabriel, Jul 2026). Doom Elite & Zombie tables are cpt-hedge placeholders and NOT yet verified — treat them as rough until we capture in-game numbers (cpt-hedge was wrong by 1,000+ VR in S1).

  • Dig-site bosses CONFIRMED — 6 tiers. SH Tier N spawns a Level 10×N mutated beast. VR: SH1=3,500 · SH2=6,000 · SH3=8,000 · SH4=9,500 · SH5=10,000 · SH6=10,500.
  • Three mutated beasts, each spawnable at every tier — the name tells you the type: Mutated Mammoth = Tank, Mutant Gorilla = Aircraft, Mutated Bear = Missile. Bring the counter squad (±20%).
  • Boss power (for the rally simulator): SH1=11.83M · SH2=14.30M · SH3=17.91M · SH4=23.04M · SH5=25.96M · SH6=28.83M. Matches cpt-hedge S2 exactly.
  • Doom Elite & Zombie = PLACEHOLDER (cpt-hedge S2 Lv1-10; Lv11+ extrapolated at +250/lv). Awaiting OWOW in-game verification — do not trust for tight calls yet.

⚔ Rally: Stronghold Boss & Doom Elite

Stronghold Bosses HAVE troop types (Aircraft/Tank/Missile ±20%). Doom Elites do NOT — only VR matters for them.
⚠ Rally rule (Last War wiki): The rally leader's VR applies to the whole rally — every member uses the leader's VR regardless of their own. The highest-VR player should always lead. [wiki source]
🏆 Slot 1 is YOU (the Initiator) — Slot 1's VR is bidirectionally synced with the top VR slider: change either and both update. Your VR becomes the floor for joiners (slots 2-5): they fight at MAX(their VR, your VR). If a joiner has higher VR, they keep their own.
💡 Squad Power is entered in Millions. Just type 17.8 and we read it as 17,800,000. No zeros to count — faster than typing the full number, and the total power shown below the rally grid will echo the exact value so you can verify.
