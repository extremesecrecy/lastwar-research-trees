How to use this calculator
  1. The 3 cards above show your current limits at a glance: what you can solo, what a rally can kill, and the power gap for the next boss level.
  2. Use the What-If Calculator below: drag the Boss Level slider to pick a target, then drag Your March Power to your current (or projected) power. The verdict updates instantly.
  3. The main chart shows 3 lines — boss march power (red), the minimum march power to solo (orange), and the minimum per-member march power to rally-kill (blue). Where your power (green dashed) crosses these lines = your limit.
  4. The rally results chart shows real battle outcomes: green = boss troops killed, red = your troops lost.

Thresholds: Solo needs ~85% of boss march power. Rally needs each member at ~65%. These come from real OWOW win/loss data, not theory.
