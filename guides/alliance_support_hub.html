Key Insight
Helps increase by +1 every 2 levels, reaching 23 at Level 35
At AC Level 23 you get 16 base helps. Each help reduces timer by 0.5% (compounding).

Time Savings Math

Alliance Center Level 23 — 16 Base Helps
16 helps × 0.5% = ~7.7% reduction (compounding)
• 8-hour build: saves ~37 minutes
• 1-day build: saves ~1 hour 51 minutes
• 3-day build: saves ~5 hours 32 minutes

With Lawyer survivors (+6 bonus helps, total 22):
• 22 helps × 0.5% = ~10.4% reduction → 8-hour build saves ~50 minutes
• 1-day build: saves ~2 hours 30 minutes
• 3-day build: saves ~7 hours 30 minutes

Note: Lawyer survivor bonus (+6 helps) is based on in-game observation and not independently verified.
