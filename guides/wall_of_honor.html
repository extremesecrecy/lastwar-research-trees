⚙️ Core Mechanics
  • Heroes must reach 5★ (five stars) to enter the Wall of Honor
  • The corresponding military building must be at Level 20 (Tank Center for tank heroes, Aircraft Center for aircraft heroes, Missile Center for missile heroes)
  • Once unlocked, spend hero shards to increase the hero's Honor Level
  • Cost: 10 shards per honor level (hero-specific shards OR universal purple shards)
  • No level cap — upgrades are infinite
  • SSR hero shards invested are refunded when that hero promotes to UR
💪 Two Types of Buffs
Type A — Per-Level Passive Buff
Every honor level gained gives +0.1% HP to ALL heroes of that troop type.

Example: Leveling any Tank hero's honor gives +0.1% HP to ALL Tank heroes per level.

APPLIES TO ALL heroes of the same type — not just the upgraded hero!
Type B — Milestone Buff (Every 50 Levels)
Every 50 honor levels, a hero-specific milestone buff activates:

UR heroes: +0.50% per 50 levels
SSR heroes: +0.25% per 50 levels
Troop Load heroes: +1.00% per 50 levels

These buffs also apply to ALL heroes of the same troop type.

🎯 Priority Strategy
Investment Priority Order
  • UR heroes give DOUBLE the buff of SSR heroes (+0.50% vs +0.25%) — always prioritize UR heroes first
  • Focus on your main squad type first (for Gabriel/Extremesecrecy: Aircraft)
  • ATK buffs are generally most impactful for DPS-focused squads
  • HP/DEF buffs help survivability — great for front-line tank heroes
  • Troop Load buffs from SR heroes (Loki, Gump, Ambolt, Kane) are surprisingly good value — +1.00% per 50 levels
  • Do NOT invest in SSR heroes you plan to promote to UR — shards refund but progress resets
💡 Key Insight
★ Squad-Wide Benefits
Every hero you level in the Wall of Honor benefits your ENTIRE squad of that type. This is one of the few systems where investing in benched heroes still helps your main team. Even an SSR hero you never use in combat will contribute passive HP and milestone buffs to every hero of the same troop type — including your top-tier UR carries.
