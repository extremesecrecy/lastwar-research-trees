1. What is the Chip Lab?

The Chip Lab is a building that lets you craft drone skill chips from materials. Chips are equipped to your drone and provide permanent combat bonuses that scale with upgrades and stars. Unlike gear or research, chip power carries across all game modes.

  • Building Unlocks: HQ Level 15 (and approximately server day 80–82)
  • Chip Lab Max Level: 35 (Levels 31–35 require Oil — Season 2+)
  • Chip Lab Lv 15: First legendary chip recipe available (Initial Move)
  • Chip Lab Lv 20: All legendary chip recipes unlocked
  • Chip Lab Lv 21–35: Power increases only (no new recipes)
  • Key rule: Always craft the highest rarity you can. Never waste materials on lower tiers.

2. Chip Types & Categories

All chips fall into 4 functional categories. Each category serves a different combat role. A balanced loadout uses chips from multiple categories.

Category Icon Color Role Best For
Attack Red Boost drone damage, expand target range DPS heroes (DVA, Kimberly, Schuyler)
Defense Blue Increase HP, armor, damage resistance Tanks (Murphy, Williams, Carlie, Lucius)
Initial Move Green Shield at battle start (~10 seconds) Opening protection for all squads
Interference Purple Extends damage duration, reduces enemy tactic damage PvP disruption, utility builds
