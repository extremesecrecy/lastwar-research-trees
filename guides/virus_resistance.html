ⓘ DATA NOTE — OWOW In-Game Verified

We initially used cpt-hedge.com's Season 1 data but discovered their numbers were off (in some cases by 1,000+ VR). We rebuilt the Doom Elite and Zombie tables using values verified directly in-game by OWOW members. Examples of cpt-hedge inaccuracies we corrected:

  • Doom Elite Lv15-22 OWOW VERIFIED (Apr 14 2026): 2750 / 3000 / 3250 / 3500 / 3750 / 4000 / 4250 / 4500. Clean +250 VR per level pattern. All other elite levels are linear extrapolation (±250/lv), cap at 10,500 from Lv46 onward.
  • Zombie Lv15-22 OWOW VERIFIED (Apr 14 2026): 2750 / 3000 / 3300 / 3400 / 3700 / 3800 / 4000 / 4200. Linear regression on the verified range: y = 201.79 × Lv − 214.29, R² = 0.989. Lv23+ extrapolated at +200 VR per level (matches regression slope), cap at 10,500 from Lv54 onward.
  • Stronghold Boss fully verified SH1-SH10 (Apr 14 2026) — see reference table below.
  • Stronghold Bosses HAVE troop types (Aircraft/Tank/Missile) — widely reported as "no type" online, but OWOW verified in-game. Counter bonus ±20%.
  • In the All Tables tab, a ✓ in the Src column marks in-game verified levels; ~ marks extrapolations.

Doom Walkers do NOT require Virus Resistance — confirmed in-game Apr 14 2026 (the Walker info panel shows only "Suggested Power", no VR line). They are strength-gated only. Walker power table rebuilt from OWOW in-game screenshots: Lv110 = 3.40M, Lv130 = 8.99M, Lv150 = 16.98M, Lv160 = 21.45M, Lv170 = 26.40M, Lv180 = 29.97M, Lv190 = 34.31M. Capture Limits

  • Max 2 Strongholds AND 2 Cities captured per day
  • Base stronghold limit: 3. Each city captured adds +1 slot (+2 for Lv6 cities)
  • City hold limit: 4 on home server + 4 across other servers = 8 total
  • Only adjacent strongholds/cities can be captured (territory must be connected)
  • Capture protection: 36 hours for strongholds, 7 days for cities

Corruptor Boss Mechanic

Strongholds start owned by the Crimson Legion, guarded by Corruptors (with troop types!). First alliance to capture fights the Corruptor once. After capture, only regular Defenders spawn — if you lose the stronghold, the Corruptor does NOT come back. Type matchup only matters for the first capture from the Legion.

⚔ Rally: Stronghold Boss & Doom Elite

Stronghold Bosses HAVE troop types (Aircraft/Tank/Missile ±20%). Doom Elites do NOT — only VR matters for them.
⚠ Rally rule (Last War wiki): The rally leader's VR applies to the whole rally — every member uses the leader's VR regardless of their own. The highest-VR player should always lead. [wiki source]
🏆 Slot 1 is YOU (the Initiator) — Slot 1's VR is bidirectionally synced with the top VR slider: change either and both update. Your VR becomes the floor for joiners (slots 2-5): they fight at MAX(their VR, your VR). If a joiner has higher VR, they keep their own.
💡 Squad Power is entered in Millions. Just type 17.8 and we read it as 17,800,000. No zeros to count — faster than typing the full number, and the total power shown below the rally grid will echo the exact value so you can verify.
