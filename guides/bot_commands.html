Player Stats & Roster Alliance + Rank

How to register: Every alliance member should run !mystats [HQ] [power] to add themselves to the roster. This data is used for Desert Storm team balancing.
!mystats
View your current stats on file.
!mystats [HQ] [power]
Set or update your HQ level and max power. Alliance is auto-detected from your role.
Example: !mystats 26 40.6M
!roster
View the full alliance roster sorted by power. Shows HQ, power, alliance tag, and team assignment.

Desert Storm Teams Alliance + Rank R4+ to assign

!teams
View Desert Storm Team A and Team B assignments with total power per team.
Any ranked alliance member can view
!team @player A
Assign a player to Team A or Team B. R4+ only.
Example: !team @Starcatcher07 B
!clearteams
Clear all team assignments for a fresh Desert Storm round. R4+ only.

R4C Desert Storm Planner R4C Official Site

Use our !roster and !teams for power balancing, then use R4C for the visual map assignments.

War & Rally R4+ Only

!rally [target]
Pings everyone with the Rally Ping role. Include a target description.
Example: !rally BaddBunnyGab base at coords 234,567
Warning: Only R4 Officers and R5 Leaders can call rallies. This pings everyone who opted into Rally Ping — don't abuse it.
