How much do tasks & goals matter? (top-tier leader reaching 80% of requirement)

Reading it

  • Big rallies can win below the requirement. At 80% you deal 40% less damage, but a 5-squad rally usually has far more power than a stronghold guardian — that's why alliances capture strongholds before they "should".
  • SH4–SH6 are close together (+1,500, +500, +500): once you can take SH3, the rest come fast.
  • Lower-tier alliances climb slowly on their own — borrow an allied high-resistance rally leader for SH3+ captures.

How to push Curse Resistance fast

Only three things raise Curse Resistance: the Curse Research Lab (Sacred Water), the Weekly Pass (+250) and the Desert Protector skill Power of Adaptation (+250 at 500+ Protectors). Everything below feeds those.

Before / at season start (alliance)

  • Pick 1–2 "resistance leaders" (most active, willing to buy the Weekly Pass / Mithril packs). They lead every stronghold rally — their resistance covers the whole rally.
  • Route Mithril & Sacred Water alliance resource tiles to the leaders.
  • Push the Alliance Center: Lv5 unlocks the Mithril Plant (34,560 Mithril/h), Lv15 the Sacred Water Hut (8,640 Sacred Water/h).

Day 1 (leader)

  • Buy the Weekly Pass: +250 resistance, a 4th Blessing Fountain, 20k Mithril, 50 Protector Horns/day.
  • Build/upgrade Blessing Fountain I with every bit of Mithril (Lv1–10 costs 243k total; output 720 Sacred Water/h per level). Fountain II unlocks when I hits Lv15.
  • Kill zombies Lv1–3 (no resistance needed) for first-kill Mithril & Sacred Water, then go up one level at a time — every new level is a fresh first-kill reward.
  • Hunt Wandering Mummy Warlords (no resistance needed) — best early Mithril.
  • Knock out every daily task / target task / season quest the moment it's available (S2 had instant ones like "kill 200 zombies").
  • Upgrade the Curse Research Lab as soon as Sacred Water allows, then claim Individual Goals after every Lab level (Season 3 → Goals → Individual) — they pay Mithril & Sacred Water back.

Every day after

  • Collect Fountains at least once every 24h — they stop producing when full (1 day of storage).
  • Doom Walker first kills, level by level (Mithril scales up — ~207k at Lv190).
  • Sandworm Crisis daily tasks → Sandworm Scales → Desert Bazaar (Mithril, Sacred Water, Protector Horns; weekly reset).
  • Gather Sacred Water fields; first capture of each city also pays Sacred Water.
  • Station Protectors until you reach 500 (+250 resistance on marches carrying them).
  • Keep rallying strongholds just below the requirement when the rally is strong — use the cards at the top to see the damage you'll lose.
