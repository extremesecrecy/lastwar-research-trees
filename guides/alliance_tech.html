🏛 What Is Alliance Technology
Server-Wide Buffs for All Members
Alliance Technology provides permanent buffs that benefit every single member of the alliance automatically. There are two main categories: Development Techs (construction speed, research speed, economy bonuses) and War Techs (combat stats, rally power, troop buffs). Techs are funded collectively by member donations using resources or diamonds. Only R4 and R5 officers can select which technology the alliance researches next.
  • All alliance members benefit from completed techs — no action required once researched
  • Two categories: Development Techs (building/research/economy) and War Techs (ATK/DEF/HP/rally)
  • Funded by member donations — contribution points earned from each donation
  • Only R4+ officers can select which tech to research next
  • Buffs stack multiplicatively with VIP, Secretary positions, and personal research
💰 Donation Mechanics
🌱 Resource Donations
Costs food, iron, or gold per donation. Earns 50 contribution points per donation (or 60 points if the tech is marked "Recommended"). Best option for F2P players.
💎 Diamond Donations
Costs 4–10 diamonds depending on the tech tier. Earns contribution points per donation. More expensive but faster progress. Use sparingly — resource donations are more efficient for F2P.
⏰ Daily Limit
Up to 50 donations per day per member. That's potentially 50 × 60 = 3,000 contribution points/day if always donating to the Recommended tech. Maxing donations daily compounds massively over time.
🎯 Strategy
Always donate to the "Recommended" tech — it gives +20% more contribution points (60 vs 50). R4 officers should always mark the current priority tech as Recommended. Encourage ALL members to hit their 50 daily donations.

🎯 R4 Donation Strategy
Priority Order for R4 Officers
  1. Always set the "Recommended" tech to what the alliance needs most — members get +20% more contribution points when donating to it
  2. Cooperative Construction first — benefits every member's building speed, accelerates HQ progression for the whole alliance
  3. Cooperative Research second — speeds up everyone's tech progress, helps the alliance unlock higher troop tiers faster
  4. War techs after cooperative buffs are well-progressed — rally ATK/DEF/HP become critical for endgame PvP
  5. Encourage members to donate daily — 50 donations/day × 30 members = massive collective progress
  6. Diamond donations are expensive — resource donations are more efficient for F2P. Only recommend diamond donations for urgent tech pushes
🔢 Quick Math
With 30 active members hitting 50 daily donations each on the Recommended tech:
30 × 50 × 60 = 90,000 contribution points per day

Even with 20 active members: 20 × 50 × 60 = 60,000 points/day. Consistency is everything — daily donations compound faster than you think.
