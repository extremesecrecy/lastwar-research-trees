Research Tree Advisor

Analyze your research progress across all 16 tech trees
  1. Go to the Research Tree Tracker and update your research levels (click each node, set your current level)
  2. Click Export CSV at the bottom of the page — this downloads your research progress
  3. Download the AI Advisor Prompt using the button below
  4. Open your preferred AI chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
  5. Upload both files — the CSV and the prompt — in a single message
  6. Ask: "Analyze my research progress and tell me what to research next"
📥 Download Research AI Prompt
What the AI will tell you: Which trees to prioritize, how many Valor Badges you need for the next critical research, what prerequisites are blocking you, and whether your research order matches the recommended 75-step path.
🏗

Building Upgrade Advisor

Analyze your building levels and plan the fastest path to your next HQ
  1. Go to the Building Planner and set your current HQ level and all building levels
  2. Click Export CSV — this downloads all 32 buildings with levels, power, and HQ requirements
  3. Download the AI Advisor Prompt using the button below
  4. Upload both files to your AI chatbot
  5. Ask: "What should I upgrade next to reach HQ 26?" or "Which buildings give the most power?"
📥 Download Building AI Prompt
What the AI will tell you: The exact chain of buildings needed for your next HQ level, which buildings give the best power-per-resource, builder queue optimization, and whether your speedup inventory can cover the upgrades.
🦸

Hero Advisor

Analyze your hero lineup, squad composition, and upgrade priorities
  1. Go to the Hero Planner and enter your hero levels, stars, skills, gear, and squad assignments
  2. Click Export CSV — this downloads all your hero data
  3. Upload the CSV to your AI chatbot along with this prompt:
# Copy and paste this into your AI chatbot along with your CSV:

You are a Last War: Survival hero advisor. I've uploaded my hero roster CSV.

The CSV columns are: Hero, Rarity (UR/SSR/SR), Type (Aircraft/Tank/Missile),
Role (DPS/Defense/Buffer), Level, Stars (0-5), Skill1/Skill2/Skill3 levels,
GearCannon/GearShield/GearChip/GearRadar levels, WeaponLv, Squad (0=bench, 1-4).

# Key game rules:
- Hero level cap = HQ x 5 - 1
- Skill cap by stars: 0★=Lv1, 1★=Lv3, 2★=Lv6, 3★=Lv10, 4★=Lv20, 5★=Lv30
- 5 same-type heroes in a squad = 20% damage bonus
- Star costs: 1★=25, 2★=50, 3★=100, 4★=300, 5★=500 shards
- DPS heroes: prioritize Cannon > Chip > Shield > Radar
- Defense heroes: prioritize Shield > Radar > Cannon > Chip
- Thursday scoring: 1 point per 2,000 Hero XP spent

Analyze my roster and tell me:
1. Squad composition analysis (synergy, type bonus)
2. Which hero to level next for maximum power gain
3. Which hero needs stars most urgently (skills at cap)
4. Gear upgrade priorities per squad
5. Thursday leveling plan if I have [X] Hero XP
What the AI will tell you: Whether your squads have full type synergy, which heroes are bottlenecked by star level, optimal XP distribution for Thursday, gear priority order, and which bench heroes to invest in next.
